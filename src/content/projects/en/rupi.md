Website for Rupi — a mobile app and system for inspection, installation and service technicians. The app lets them accept jobs, record work details, talk to customers and reach documentation straight from the field.

The site is built as a classic SaaS presentation: industry solutions split by technician type, a breakdown of the main features, a "how Rupi makes your work easier" section, pricing and a blog.

It also carries the legal pages (GDPR, terms) and a contact form for demo requests.
