Company website for Green Blue, which advises producers and importers of specified products on meeting their statutory waste management obligations.

Services are listed separately — consultancy, audit and verification, record keeping, outsourcing, electronic record keeping and authorised representative — because clients typically pick just one of them.

The site also holds a legislation section, a register, an FAQ, news and access to the client zone. An English version is available.
