### Starting point

NTM Cleaning does deep cleaning of upholstery and carpets, leather care and interior car detailing at the customer's location across four regions of western Slovakia. The old website ran on a ready-made theme with the Elementor page builder, and on mobile its main content appeared after more than eleven seconds.

### What was done

- A custom WordPress theme without a page builder.
- Services, pricing, projects, counters, FAQ and contact on one page in a logical order; subpages that only repeated text were dropped and the old URLs redirected.
- Phone number and free quote request always within reach; the form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile and each submission is tracked in GA4.
- Business data for Google, a sitemap, Search Console and an llms.txt file set up from day one.
- A complete set of site icons: ICO, PNG in three sizes, apple-touch-icon and a web manifest.

### Result

Comparison in Google PageSpeed Insights, median of three runs per site:

- mobile performance: 60 → 99
- largest contentful paint on mobile: 11.4 s → 1.7 s
- layout shift (CLS) on mobile: 0.132 → 0
- desktop performance: 72 → 100
- accessibility: 85 → 100
- SEO and best practices of the new site: 100

These are lab numbers; I will add data from real visitors once Search Console has collected it.
