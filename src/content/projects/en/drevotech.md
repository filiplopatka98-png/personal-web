E-shop for DREVOTECH s.r.o. The company makes two entirely different product groups, so the homepage works as a hub — visitors pick a division first and only then enter the matching catalogue.

The **coffins** branch offers four-edge luxury caskets, urns and funeral decorations. The **windows** branch presents DAKO wooden windows and doors, including downloadable material sheets.

Both branches share a common WooCommerce base and a B2B partner registration.
