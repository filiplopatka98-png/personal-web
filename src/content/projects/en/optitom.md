Website for the OPTITOM eye clinic, which provides ophthalmological care for children and adults.

The site serves two kinds of visit — a patient who wants to book, and a patient checking opening hours or a price. Online booking and opening hours are therefore reachable straight from the homepage and repeated in the footer, with a separate link for ordering an e-prescription.

Content splits into eye examinations, diagnostics and treatment, pricing, news, careers and an FAQ covering what to know before a first appointment.
