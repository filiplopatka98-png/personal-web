Website for Senset — an automated dispatch system for taxi companies that assigns rides without a human dispatcher, with a driver app and a passenger app.

Content is organised around three roles — owners, drivers and passengers — because each of them uses the system differently. The homepage explains the order flow (phone, scheduled web booking, passenger app) and the automatic assignment that follows.

Taxi company testimonials, pricing, a blog, videos, a driver manual, passenger app help and a webinar complete the site. It runs in both Slovak and Czech editions.
