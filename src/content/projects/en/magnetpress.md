Online portal for the publisher MAGNET PRESS SLOVAKIA, whose titles include atm, Letectví + kosmonautika, Modelář, AeroHobby, Šikovníček, Moje zdravie, Moja psychológia and F.O.O.D.

Each title gets its own branch with sections (for atm: current topic, land systems, aviation, naval, defence industry, history), the current issue, an archive, subscriptions and advertising.

The portal links the reading experience to subscription orders and points to the publisher's SK and CZ e-shops.
