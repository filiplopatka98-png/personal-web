Institutional website for the St Svorad Zobor Specialist Hospital in Nitra, which provides medical and pharmacy care.

The site has to serve two distinct audiences — patients looking for a department, clinic, opening hours or pricing, and the general public looking for legally mandated disclosures. Navigation therefore splits into a "Hospital" branch (basic data, history, org structure, annual reports, orders, invoices, public procurement, ethics committee) and quick links to departments and clinics.

News, a careers section and a contact form round it out.
