Company website for DAPEL s.r.o., an authorised installer of XPEL paint protection films in Slovakia — branches in Prešov (HQ), Košice, Ružomberok, Žarnovica, Poprad and Bratislava.

The core of the site is a wrap configurator with four packages (Štandart+, Premium, Komplet, Exkluzív). The package overview links straight into the configurator with the choice pre-filled, so the inquiry arrives with a concrete package already attached.

The site also presents the film product range, references, a video gallery of finished jobs and contact details for each branch.
