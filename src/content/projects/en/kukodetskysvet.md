Custom-built site for a children's indoor playroom and café in Piešťany, built without WordPress or third-party services — its own PHP 8 and MySQL backend, with a vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend rendered through PHP templates. One-page design in a pastel color palette.

It includes a booking system for children's birthday parties (3 packages), a custom CMS for the owner to edit content, and an email pipeline via PHPMailer + SMTP for confirmations and cron-scheduled reminders. Booking flow: form → pending row in the database → admin approval → emails to both customer and admin → a reminder before the event.
