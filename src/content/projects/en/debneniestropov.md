The company rents out slab formwork and clamps to construction firms and private builders around Trnava and Piešťany, Slovakia. It had no website or domain before, so this was not a redesign but its first channel for inquiries.

The brief was simple: a visitor should see within seconds what is on offer and what it costs, and be able to send an inquiry straight away. That is why the site is a single page. The price, from €0.25 per m² per day, sits at the top, followed by the equipment on offer, a rough price calculator, a four-step process and a FAQ.

Inquiries come in through a no-obligation quote form. The customer fills in the ceiling area, the building location, the start date and the material, and can attach a drawing (PDF, DWG, DXF, JPG, PNG or ZIP up to 10 MB). The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, so visitors do not have to solve image captchas.

The site runs on a custom WordPress theme without a page builder, built on my Lopatka Starter base. It includes structured data for the services and the FAQ, and an llms.txt file. A submitted form and a click on the phone number are tracked in Google Analytics 4 once the visitor accepts analytics cookies.

In a simulated PageSpeed test the site scores 100 on mobile with an LCP of 1.7 s, and 100 on desktop with an LCP of 0.5 s.
