English-language website for The Christmas Creations Company, which handles commercial Christmas decorating for businesses in Dublin and across Ireland — from trees through retail displays to full corporate décor.

The site has an online configurator that lets a client assemble a complete decoration package, plus a "Why Rent" section explaining the rental-over-purchase model.

A project gallery and client testimonials round it out. The business also serves Northern Ireland, so the contact section separates IE and NI phone lines.
