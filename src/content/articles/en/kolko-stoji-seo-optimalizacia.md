The answer to "how much does SEO cost" is always "it depends". Here is a breakdown of what makes up the price, what I charge and what makes sense for a small business.

## SEO price list: indicative prices

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Basic SEO audit of a small website (up to about 10 pages) | €99 |
| Fixes based on the audit | €30/h, hour estimate upfront |
| Larger sites and online stores | quoted individually |
| Monthly SEO management | not offered yet |

Other providers' prices vary with the scope of work, so I don't list figures here that I couldn't back up. When comparing offers, ask what exactly is included in the price.

## What makes up the price

- **Audit.** Scope sets the price: the more pages, the longer the analysis. For a small site it is enough to check indexing, the main technical errors and titles.
- **Fixes.** Hourly work from the audit's task list: technical issues, titles, redirects. You get an hour estimate upfront.
- **Content.** New pages and articles that target what people actually search for.
- **Monthly management.** Regular articles and ongoing tweaks. It only makes sense when search demand in your field is large.

## When to expect results

First effects usually show after 3 to 6 months, later for new domains. Anyone who promises rankings within a week is not telling the truth.

## SEO in Slovakia: one-off foundation or monthly management

If search demand in your field is small, monthly management makes little sense. A one-off foundation is enough: technical basics, correct titles, a few quality pages and a Google Business Profile. The process is in the [SEO optimization guide](/en/blog/seo-optimalizacia-webu-navod/).

## What is in the €99 audit

The audit price is low because the scope is clearly defined: indexing, main technical errors, titles and meta descriptions, and a prioritised task list. Fixes based on the audit are billed hourly (€30/h) with an estimate upfront. Details are on the [services page](/en/services/).

## What to watch out for

An offer without an audit, without a list of concrete tasks and with a promise of rankings.
