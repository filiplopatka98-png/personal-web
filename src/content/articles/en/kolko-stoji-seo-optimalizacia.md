The answer to "how much does SEO cost" is always "it depends". Here is a breakdown of what makes up the price and what to choose.

## What makes up the price

- **Audit.** According to a [Slovak SEO blog](https://loudavymkrokem.cz/sk/kolko-stoji-seo/), audits cost from €200 to €2,000 depending on site size, with an average of €800.
- **Content.** A quality SEO article costs around €100 to €200.
- **Monthly management.** Agency packages start at around [€600 a month](https://loudavymkrokem.cz/sk/?p=77984), which covers basic fixes and 3 to 4 articles.

## When to expect results

First effects usually show after 3 to 6 months, later for new domains. Anyone who promises rankings within a week is not telling the truth.

## What makes sense for a small business

If search demand in your field is small, monthly management makes little sense. A one-off foundation is enough: technical basics, correct titles, a few quality pages and a Google Business Profile. The process is in the [SEO optimization guide](/en/blog/seo-optimalizacia-webu-navod/).
