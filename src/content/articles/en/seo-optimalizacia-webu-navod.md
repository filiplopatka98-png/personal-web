SEO is a sequence of steps. The most common mistake is writing articles for a site that Google has barely crawled. This guide goes from the foundation to the content, so you don't do work that leads nowhere. For a deeper technical breakdown, see the [technical SEO checklist](/en/blog/seo-checklist-co-pomaha/).

## 1. Check that Google can see your site

In Search Console (a "domain" property) open *Indexing → Pages*. If many URLs are marked "Discovered, currently not indexed", the problem is not your content but crawling. Submit your most important pages via *URL Inspection → Request indexing*. The daily quota is small, so start with the homepage, your services and your best articles.

## 2. Technical foundation

- a submitted `sitemap.xml`,
- a single version of the site (https, with or without www) with no redirect chains,
- no accidental `noindex`,
- `hreflang` on multilingual sites,
- mobile experience and speed, see [LCP over 2.5 s: causes](/en/blog/lcp-nad-2-5s-pricin/).

## 3. Keywords by intent

Find out what people actually search for. The Google Ads keyword planner is free. Search volumes in Slovakia are small, so the goal is to rank first for a specific topic, not for a broad one. "Online store development price" beats "online store".

## 4. One page, one topic

A title of about 60 characters with the main keyword, a single H1 and a meta description that earns the click. Two pages targeting the same keyword weaken each other.

## 5. Content better than the competition

Go through the top 10 results and write what they are missing: a concrete procedure, numbers, first-hand experience. One good article beats ten average ones.

## 6. Internal links

Link every new article to a related service and back. It helps Google understand your structure and helps readers find the next step.

## 7. Measure and adjust

After 4 to 6 weeks, look in Search Console at queries with many impressions and a low CTR. Rewrite the title and meta description there. It is the cheapest change that often brings the most clicks.
